## Step-by-Step Upload Instructions (Desktop YouTube Studio)

### Step 1: Open YouTube Studio & Upload
1. Go to [studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com).
2. Click the **Create** button (top right) ➔ **Upload videos**.
3. Drag & drop the `.mp4` video file into the window.

### Step 2: Apply Title & Description
1. Open the corresponding `upload_metadata.md` file in the short's folder.
2. **Title**: Copy Option 1 (or your preferred option). Always ensure `#shorts` is in the title.
3. **Description**: Copy and paste the pre-written description.

### Step 3: Select Thumbnail Frame
- YouTube Shorts automatically generate thumbnail frames from the video.
- In the YouTube mobile app (or Studio), select the frame with the boldest visual (e.g. chart collapse or clear text caption).

### Step 4: Audience Setting
- Under **Audience**, select: **"No, it's not made for kids"**.
- Leave age restriction off (safe for general audiences).

### Step 5: The Crucial Funnel Step — "Related Video"
> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is how Shorts get you thousands of views on your long videos!
1. Still on the **Video Details** page, scroll down to the **Related video** field.
2. Click the pencil icon / search bar.
3. Select your flagship episode: **"Why Do Stock Market Crashes Actually Happen? (1929, 2008, 2020)"**.
4. Now, any viewer on mobile will see a clickable button at the bottom of the Short leading directly to EP01.

### Step 6: Tags & Category
1. Click **SHOW MORE** at the bottom of the details page.
2. Copy the comma-separated tags from `upload_metadata.md` and paste them into the **Tags** box.
3. Set **Category** to **Education** (or *Film & Animation / Science & Technology*).
4. Set **Video Language** to **English**.

### Step 7: Visibility & Schedule
- If publishing immediately: Choose **Public** ➔ Click **Publish**.
- If scheduling (e.g., Short 2 tomorrow at 16:00 NY time):
  1. Click **Schedule**.
  2. Choose the date.
  3. Select **4:00 PM** (16:00) with your timezone set to **(GMT-04:00) Eastern Time (US & Canada)**.
  4. Click **Schedule**.

### Step 8: Post-Publish Action (First 5 Minutes)
1. Open the published Short on YouTube.
2. Copy the **Pinned Comment** from `upload_metadata.md`.
3. Post it as a comment on your video.
4. Click the three dots beside your comment ➔ select **Pin**.
5. Give your own comment a heart ❤️.
