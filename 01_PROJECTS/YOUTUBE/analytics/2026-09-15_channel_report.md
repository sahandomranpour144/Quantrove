### Empirical Observations:

1. **The Critical 0–10% Drop Window (The Universal Cliff)**:
   - **EP01 (`oJstBJgNAi4`)**:
     - 0:04 (1%): 69.0%
     - 0:19 (5%): 44.8%
     - 0:38 (10%): **31.0%**
     - *Total loss in first 38 seconds: 69.0% of all viewers.*
   - **EP02 (`3I84-kRga0s`)**:
     - 0:05 (1%): 90.4%
     - 0:24 (5%): 51.0%
     - 0:49 (10%): **34.6%**
     - *Total loss in first 49 seconds: 65.4% of all viewers.*

2. **The "Surviving Cohort" Stability (The Plateau)**:
   - Once past the 10% mark, the viewer curve levels off into an unusually flat line:
     - **EP01**: Remains between **27.6% and 34.5%** for almost the entire middle body (from 1:13 to 5:12, representing 70% of the entire video).
     - **EP02**: Stays between **18.3% and 22.1%** steadily from 1:37 through 6:15.
   - **Diagnosis**: There is **no structural mid-video pacing issue**. The visual storytelling (Manim math + Flow scenes) holds audiences remarkably well once they buy into the premise. Viewers who make it past the opening stay for the entire breakdown.

3. **End-Screen Drop**:
   - Both videos see a dip in the final 5% (conclusion/outro), dropping to ~10%–17% as callouts appear. This is normal viewer behavior on educational YouTube.

4. **Strategic Validation**:
   - This exact data pattern confirms why the newly established **[Hook Discipline Rule](../../../.claude/rules/hook-discipline.md)** is vital: **Every single percent of retention gained in the first 5–8 seconds directly multiplies the size of the flat plateau that watches the entire video.**
