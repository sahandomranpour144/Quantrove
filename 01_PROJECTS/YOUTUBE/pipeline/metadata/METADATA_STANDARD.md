# Quantrove YouTube Metadata Standard

## 1. Title Rules
- **Long-Form Titles**: Exactly 6–7 words and strictly `< 60` characters. High CTR, clear promise, no clickbait deception.

## 2. Default Description Block
```text
Quantrove: data-driven documentaries on AI, markets, and the systems behind them. Every episode is built from primary data.

Channel: youtube.com/@Quantrove
Playlists: {PILLAR_PLAYLIST_LINK}

Educational content only. Not financial, investment, or trading advice. Markets involve risk.
```

## 4. Shorts Description Template
```text
{HOOK}
Full breakdown: {long link}

Educational content only. Not financial, investment, or trading advice. Markets involve risk.

#Quantrove #{PillarHashtag} #{TopicHashtag}
```

## 5. Tag Architecture & Defaults
- **Rules**: Exact topic tag first. Target `<= 300` characters total including commas; hard ceiling `<= 500` characters.
- **Core (Every Video)**: `Quantrove, quantitative finance, finance explained, financial markets, how markets work`
- **Pillar 1 (AI & ML Real World)**: `machine learning explained, how AI works, real world AI`
- **Pillar 2 (AI + Finance + Trading)**: `AI trading, algorithmic trading, quantitative trading, backtesting`
- **Pillar 3 (Finance Simplified)**: `finance simplified, investing explained, market mechanics, liquidity`
- **Pillar 4 (Data Stories)**: `data storytelling, stock market history, market data analysis`
- **Per Video (5–8 tags)**: exact topic phrase (first), 2 variants, acronym plus full name, common misspelling, 1 broad term.
- **Shorts Tags**: Core tags plus 3–4 specific topic tags.

## 6. Hashtags
Exactly 3 hashtags per video: `#Quantrove #{PillarHashtag} #{TopicHashtag}`.
