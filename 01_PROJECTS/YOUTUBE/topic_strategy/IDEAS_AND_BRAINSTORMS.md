## OBSERVATIONS
*Interesting things noticed in markets, AI, technology, data, etc.*

- **Speed of Light in Media**: Light travels ~30% slower in glass fiber (~200,000 km/s) than through air microwaves (~300,000 km/s). This physical constant created a multi-million-dollar microwave relay construction race across the Allegheny Mountains.
- **Alpha Decay Acceleration**: In the 1990s, a quantitative anomaly had an alpha half-life of 12–18 months. Today, automated factor scanning compresses alpha half-life to days or even hours.
- **Passive Index Distortions**: Over 50% of US equities are now held in passive index funds and target-date retirement funds. Inflows are price-insensitive, creating structural valuation distortions that quantitative funds harvest.
- **Retail Options Boom**: Zero-day-to-expiration (0DTE) options now account for over 45% of S&P 500 options trading volume, introducing new gamma squeeze mechanics into daily market closes.
