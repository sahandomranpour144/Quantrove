## 1. Purpose & Conceptual Foundation

The Quantrove Topic Strategy system provides a rigorous, data-driven framework for ideating, evaluating, selecting, and architecting long-form documentary episodes.

### The Fundamental Architectural Distinction
To prevent strategic confusion and preserve long-term channel flexibility, Quantrove establishes a clear distinction between channel scope and seasonal narrative:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{\textbf{Pillars Answer:}} &\quad \text{"What kind of content is Quantrove?"} \quad \implies \text{\textbf{PERMANENT}} \\
\text{\textbf{Seasons Answer:}} &\quad \text{"What larger question connects this group of episodes?"} \quad \implies \text{\textbf{TEMPORARY}}
\end{aligned}$$

1. **The Four Pillars are Permanent**: They define the editorial identity, audience scope, and playlist organization of the channel. They never rotate out or expire.
2. **Seasons are Temporary Narrative Groupings**: They connect a cluster of episodes around a shared investigation, intellectual tension, or market phenomenon.
3. **Future Seasons are Never Predetermined**: No fixed sequence of future seasons is locked in advance. Each new season is designed only at season boundaries after reviewing completed work and empirical evidence.

## 2. Permanent Channel Architecture: The Four Pillars

The channel's permanent editorial backbone consists of four playlists, balanced via a disciplined weekly rotation:

| # | Pillar (Playlist) | Editorial Scope & Focus | Target Cadence |

| 1 | 🤖 **AI & ML Real World** | Concrete AI applications, machine learning architectures explained, real-world impact, technical limits. | 1 pillar/week rotation |

| 4 | 📊 **Data Stories** | Empirical historical datasets, counterintuitive findings, systemic market anomalies, forensic data deep-dives. | 1 pillar/week rotation |

### Production Cadence Rules:
- **Weekly Volume**: 2 long-form documentary episodes + 5–6 Shorts per week.
- **Weekly Pillar Focus**: One pillar (playlist) per week, rotating systematically across all four pillars to build balanced library depth.
- **Outro Decoupling (Rule from EP06 onward)**: An episode's outro must **NOT** announce or tease the specific next episode's topic. Outros use generic, high-retention CTAs and playlist recommendations only. This prevents rigid continuity bottlenecks and allows flexible scheduling.

## 3. Quantrove Topic Selection Framework (100-Point System)

Every proposed long-form video topic undergoes evaluation against six weighted strategic dimensions, yielding a final normalized score out of 100 points.

### The Six Evaluation Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Weight | Strategic Focus | Scoring Guidance (0.0 to 10.0) |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Curiosity / Click Potential** | 25% | Hook strength, counterintuitive premise, packaging potential (title + thumbnail), tension. | 0 = Generic/boring; 5 = Moderate interest; 10 = Irresistible paradox |
| 2 | **Season / Narrative Connection** | 20% | Compatibility with active season, existing channel narrative, and accumulated previous work. *(If no future season exists, marked `TBD — Season Boundary Review` and scored on channel narrative fit).* | 0 = Disconnected; 5 = Loose thematic tie; 10 = Essential narrative or pillar anchor |
| 3 | **Data Availability** | 15% | Access to verified empirical datasets (FRED, SEC filings, tick data, order books, academic papers). | 0 = Speculation; 5 = Secondhand estimates; 10 = Verified tick-level / primary data |
| 4 | **Visual Potential** | 15% | Opportunities for clean Manim math animations, vector charts, and cinematic Flow B-roll clips. | 0 = Talking head; 5 = Static charts; 10 = Dynamic 3D Manim / spatial visuals |
| 5 | **Educational Value** | 15% | High institutional substance, structural clarity, zero superficial clickbait or hype. | 0 = Surface fluff; 5 = Textbook summary; 10 = Deep institutional mechanics revealed |
| 6 | **Evergreen Lifespan** | 10% | Enduring relevance across 2–5 years; focuses on timeless mathematical or market laws. | 0 = Daily news cycle; 5 = 6-month trend; 10 = Timeless structural truth |
| | **TOTAL** | **100%** | | |

### Scoring Methodology & Normalization
$$\text{Total Score} = \frac{(C \times 25) + (S \times 20) + (D \times 15) + (V \times 15) + (E \times 15) + (L \times 10)}{10}$$

### Decision Interpretation Tiers

| Score Tier | Classification | Recommended Operational Action |
|---|---|---|
| **90.0 – 100.0** | **Exceptional Candidate** | **Fast-Track Priority**: Immediate greenlight consideration for packaging research, scripting, and shot listing. |
| **80.0 – 89.9** | **Strong Candidate** | **Standard Greenlight**: Strong contender; proceed to research validation and packaging design. |
| **70.0 – 79.9** | **Strategic Review Required** | **Refinement Needed**: Diagnose low-scoring dimensions (e.g., weak curiosity hook or poor data availability) and rework premise. |
| **< 70.0** | **Normally Reject / Rework** | **Archive / Re-scope**: Return to Idea Inbox or fundamentally reconceptualize core thesis. |

> **⚠️ Governance Principle**:  
> Scores provide planning guidance, not an automated mandate. A topic scoring 95/100 is not automatically produced; Sahand (CEO) retains sole authority on all production greenlights.

## 5. Episode-to-Episode Narrative Continuity

A hallmark of Quantrove's documentary quality is organic narrative continuity without rigid scheduling locks:
- **The Callback**: Episodes reference foundational insights, mathematical concepts, or historical case studies established in prior videos (e.g., calling back to the order book queues from EP03 or reflexivity from EP04).
- **Decoupled Outro CTAs**: From EP06 onward, outros do not tease a specific future topic title. Instead, they direct viewers to relevant pillar playlists or logical companion videos, ensuring evergreen shelf-life.
- **Playlist Retention**: Viewers completing an episode are systematically funnelled into curated pillar playlists.

4. **Promotion Criteria**: Ideas exhibiting strong curiosity and viable data angles are promoted from `IDEA_INBOX` to `TOPIC_DATABASE` for formal 100-point scoring.

## 8. Topic Evaluation Workflow

When moving an idea from Brainstorm/Inbox to formal evaluation:
1. **Assign Topic ID**: Assign sequential `QT-XXX` identifier.
2. **Define Core Question**: Crystallize the premise into a single provocative, specific human question.
3. **Assign Permanent Pillar**: Categorize strictly under one of the 4 official pillars (*AI & ML Real World*, *AI + Finance + Trading*, *Finance Simplified*, *Data Stories*).
4. **Verify Data Sources**: Verify primary data (FRED, SEC, tick data, academic papers). If unverified, Data Availability score must be $\le 5$.
5. **Identify Visual Anchors**: Specify what Manim will animate (equations, distributions, order books) and what Flow will generate (cinematic atmosphere).
6. **Compute 100-Point Score**: Fill raw scores $0–10$ across all 6 dimensions.
7. **Season Alignment**: Assign to active season (Season 1) if approved for near-term production; otherwise designate as `TBD — Season Boundary Review`.
8. **Verify Four-Pillar Balance**: Confirm that producing this topic maintains balanced pillar representation.

## 9. The Formal Season-End Review Model

Future seasons are **never predetermined**. Season 2 will be designed only at the formal Season Boundary Review triggered exclusively when Sahand decides to conclude Season 1.

### The 7-Step Season Boundary Review Process:

```
[A. Review Completed Episodes] ──► [B. Review Available Evidence] ──► [C. Review Narrative & Callbacks]
                                                                                │
[F. Generate Candidate Architectures] ◄── [E. Review Idea Inventory] ◄── [D. Review Pillar Balance]
                 │
                 ▼
     [🛑 G. CEO SEASON GATE] ──► Sahand selects Season Theme, Question & Episode Architecture
```

- **Step A: Review Completed Episodes**: Conduct a comprehensive retrospective on what Season 1 actually covered, identifying thematic strengths and viewer favorites.
- **Step B: Review Available Evidence**: Analyze empirical performance data that actually exists:
  - Views, watch time, and average percentage viewed (retention curves).
  - CTR and impressions where data is available in YouTube Studio.
  - Subscriber conversion rates per episode.
  - Qualitative feedback: recurring viewer questions, confusion points, and top comments.
  - *Strict rule*: Never invent or assume unavailable metrics.
- **Step C: Review Narrative & Callbacks**: Identify the strongest narrative threads, unresolved paradoxes, and natural follow-up questions raised across previous episodes.
- **Step D: Review Pillar Balance**: Audit the distribution across the 4 permanent pillars, confirming that all pillars achieved sufficient depth (minimum 3 longs per pillar verified).
- **Step E: Review Idea Inventory**: Scan `IDEA_INBOX`, `BRAINSTORM`, and `TOPIC_DATABASE` for clustered concepts, high-scoring backlog topics, and emerging financial/AI themes.
- **Step F: Generate Candidate Next-Season Architectures**: Formulate 2–3 distinct candidate season proposals (each with a theme, central question, and tentative episode grouping). Do **not** automatically select or commit to any proposal.
- **Step G: CEO Season Gate**: Sahand (CEO) reviews candidate proposals and makes the final decision on:
  1. Season Title & Working Theme
  2. Central Strategic Question
  3. Episode Architecture & Scope
  4. Pillar Distribution Plan
  *Only after Sahand's formal sign-off are topics committed to the new season.*

## 10. Rules for Turning an Idea into an Episode

Once a topic receives **CEO Approval**, it must follow the Quantrove production pipeline:

1. **Packaging First (Rule L1)**: Lock 3 high-CTR title variations (<60 characters) and the thumbnail visual concept before writing a single word of the script.
2. **Hook Discipline (Rule L2)**: The script's first 5 seconds must state the specific question or paradox and include at least 2 title keywords spoken and visible.
3. **Pace Mode Statement (Rule L3)**: Deliver pace statement within 5–20s; promised runtime must match measured runtime within $\pm 1$ minute.
4. **Documentary Chapter Quad (Rule L4 & Script Humanization Rule 2)**: Structure every chapter into:
   $$\text{Human Question} \longrightarrow \text{Mystery / Problem} \longrightarrow \text{Data Reveal} \longrightarrow \text{Consequence \& Meaning}$$
5. **Loop Ledger Tracking (Rule L5)**: Maintain 4–5 open curiosity loops with the first partial payoff by 90s.
6. **Script Humanization Pass (Rule 3.11)**: Pass the 4-pass humanization audit (`quantrove-script-humanizer`) before submitting the script to Sahand for Gate 1 approval.
