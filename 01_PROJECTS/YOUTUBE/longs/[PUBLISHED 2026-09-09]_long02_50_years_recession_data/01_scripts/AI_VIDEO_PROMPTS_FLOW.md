# EP02 AI Video Generation Blueprint (Gemini & Flow Prompts)
## "What Does 50 Years of Data Say About Recessions?"

This guide contains **director-level scene instructions and copy-paste prompts** to generate 8–10 second cinematic video clips using **Gemini** (for prompt refining/ideas) and **Google Flow / AI Video Generators** (Veo, Kling, Luma, Runway, Pika, etc.).

All prompts are tailored to match **Quantrove's dark documentary aesthetic** (moody contrast, deep navy/black shadows `#0B0E14`, cinematic anamorphic lighting, and high-stakes realism).

---

## Quick Generation Rules
* **Duration**: Generate **8 to 10 seconds** per clip.
* **Aspect Ratio**: Always select **`16:9`** (Landscape / YouTube Desktop format).
* **CapCut Placement**: Drop these video clips on **Track V1** as B-roll cutaways, or on **Track V2** with 30–50% opacity over animated charts to create a rich, layered multi-dimensional feel.

---

* **Scene Concept**: Wall Street Panic / Red Ticker Glow
* **Placement in CapCut**: Cutaway right when the voiceover says *"the news was screaming that the world was ending"*.
* **Camera Movement**: Slow motion push-in with shallow depth of field.

A lone trader in a crisp white dress shirt with loosened tie stands staring up at towering electronic stock ticker boards flashing falling red percentage numbers. Subtle atmospheric smoke haze, anamorphic lens flares, 8k resolution, photorealistic cinematic documentary style, high contrast dark theme, 24fps --ar 16:9
