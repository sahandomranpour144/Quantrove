## 🔀 3. Transitions (Simple, Clean & Professional)

Avoid fancy 3D spinning transitions or cartoon effects. A top-tier financial documentary relies on **just 3 clean transition types**:

### The 3 Master Transitions in CapCut Desktop:
1. **Mix / Cross Dissolve** (`0.3s – 0.5s`): The bread and butter. Seamlessly blends Manim charts and AI cutaways.
2. **Dip to Black / Black Fade** (`0.4s`): Used between major chapter/scene breaks to give the viewer time to absorb complex concepts.
3. **White Flash** (`0.15s`): Super fast burst used only on violent market moments (2008 Lehman freeze, Volcker rate shock).

### Where to Apply Transitions:

* **Scene 1 to Scene 2 (`00:43`)**: **Dip to Black** (`0.4s`) — Marks the transition from Hook to Theory.
* **Between Manim Charts & Gemini AI Images (e.g. `00:20`, `01:12`, `02:18`, `03:05`, `03:58`, `05:10`, `06:15`)**:
  * Apply **"Dissolve"** (or **"Mix"**) with duration **`0.4s`**.
  * *How to do it in CapCut*: Click **"Transitions"** at the top left → Under **"Basic"**, drag **"Dissolve"** directly between the two clips on your timeline.
* **Chronological Era Cuts in Scene 4 (`03:15`, `03:30`, `03:42`, `04:08`)**:
  * Apply **"Push Left"** or **"Slide Left"** (`0.3s`) to visually communicate scrubbing forward through 50 years of time!
* **Scene 5 to Scene 6 (`05:39`)**: **Dip to Black** (`0.4s`).
* **Scene 6 to Scene 7 (`06:56`)**: **Dip to Black** (`0.4s`).
* **End of Video (`08:06.50`)**: **Fade to Black** (`0.8s`).

## ✍️ 4. On-Screen Texts, Titles & Lower Thirds

Use text sparingly to emphasize key statistics and anchor the viewer's memory.

### Master Text Style Settings (CapCut Right-Hand Panel)
* **Font**: `Montserrat`, `Inter`, or `System` (Bold).
* **Case**: UPPERCASE for titles, Title Case for subtitles.
* **Color Palette**:
  * Primary Text: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  * Data / Key Numbers: Cyan / Neon Teal (`#06B6D4` or `#00F2FE`) or Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`)
  * Bearish / Warning / Myth: Crimson Red (`#EF4444`)
  * Bullish / Recovery / Fact: Emerald Green (`#10B981`)
* **Text Shadow / Outline**:
  * Shadow: Black, Opacity `80%`, Blur `15`, Distance `5` (ensures readability over any background).
* **In Animation**: Under "Animation" → "In", choose **"Fade In"** or **"Slide Up"** (Duration: `0.3s`).
* **Out Animation**: Under "Animation" → "Out", choose **"Fade Out"** (Duration: `0.3s`).

## 💬 5. Captions (Auto-Captions in 60 Seconds)

Auto-captions boost watch time significantly because many viewers watch with low or muted volume. CapCut makes this automatic:

### Step-by-Step 1-Click Captions Setup:
1. Click **"Text"** in the top navigation bar.
2. In the left panel, click **"Auto captions"**.
3. Select Language: **"English"**.
4. Click **"Create"**. *(CapCut will transcribe the Orus voiceover in ~15 seconds and place all subtitles on a dedicated caption track).*

### Caption Styling (Apply to All with 1 Click):
1. Click on **any caption block** on the timeline.
2. In the top right inspector window, make sure **"Apply to all"** is checked (it is checked by default).
3. **Font**: Select `Montserrat` or `Proxima Nova` or `System`.
4. **Style**: Click the **"B"** icon (Bold) and click the **"TT"** icon (Uppercase).
5. **Font Size**: Set to **`10` or `11`** *(do NOT make them giant; they shouldn't obstruct the Manim data axes)*.
6. **Position**: In the Basic tab, set **Y position to `-360` to `-380`** so captions sit in the lower-third, comfortably below all charts.
7. **Preset Template (Optional Modern Polish)**: In the **"Captions"** or **"Text Templates"** tab, choose the **"Word Highlight"** template (where the spoken word dynamically lights up in Yellow or Cyan as the voiceover speaks).

## 🎨 6. Visual Polish & Effects (Simple 1-Click Polish)

You don't need complex motion graphics. These 3 simple CapCut techniques make the entire video feel like a high-budget studio production:

### Technique A: Ken Burns / Slow Drift on Static Images (1 Click!)
For static Gemini AI images (e.g. `00:20` trading floor, `01:12` boardroom, `03:05` gas station, `03:58` Lehman worker, `06:15` empty office):
* Click the image clip on the timeline.
* In the right panel, go to **Animation** → **Combo**.
* Select **"Zoom 1"** (or **"Slow Zoom"**).
* Set the duration to cover the full clip.
* *Result*: The image smoothly pushes in, eliminating flat static pauses!

### Technique B: Cinematic Vignette (Gives Deep Contrast)
* Go to top menu: **Effects** → **Video Effects**.
* In the search box, type **"Vignette"**.
* Drag the Vignette effect onto **Track V4** (the topmost track).
* Drag the ends of the effect bar so it spans across the entire 8-minute timeline.
* In the right panel, set the slider to **`15 – 20%`**.
* *Result*: Subtle darkening around the edges focuses the viewer's eyes directly onto the center charts.

### Technique C: Subtle Screen Shake on Dramatic Shocks (0.4s Burst)
* At `00:20` (trading floor panic) and `03:42` (2008 Lehman collapse):
* Go to **Effects** → **Video Effects** → search **"Camera Shake"** (or **"Shake"**).
* Drag the effect directly over the 0.4s moment of impact.
* Adjust settings in right panel: **Speed: `15`**, **Intensity: `10`** (keep it gentle, not dizzying).
* *Result*: Adds instant physical impact to financial crashes!

### Technique D: Global Color Adjustment Layer
To give the entire video a uniform, punchy, high-contrast look:
* Click **"Adjustment"** in the top menu (or select all clips).
* Drag a **Default Adjustment Layer** across the timeline on Track V4.
* Set these subtle values:
  * **Contrast**: `+8`
  * **Saturation**: `+5`
  * **Sharpness**: `+10`
  * **Shadows**: `-5`
* *Result*: Blacks become deeper, glowing Manim cyan lines pop off the screen, and AI images look crisp and cinematic.
