# EP02 Strategy & Narrative Blueprint

**Video Title**: What 50 Years of Recession Data Actually Show  
**Pillar**: 📊 Data Stories (Pillar 4)  
**Target Runtime**: ~7:15 (approx. 1,050 spoken words)  
**Voice**: ElevenLabs "Adam"  

---

## 1. The Hook (First 30 Seconds)

### Spoken Hook (0:00–0:25)
> "In almost every recession over the last fifty years, the single best day to invest in the stock market happened when the news was screaming that the world was ending.
> 
> Most people believe recessions and market crashes move in lockstep. But when you look at five decades of actual economic data, you discover something completely counterintuitive: the market almost always starts a violent recovery months *before* the recession is even close to over. 
> 
> So what actually happens during a recession? How long do they really last? And why does almost everyone get the data completely backwards?"

### Visual Hook
- Fast zoom onto a 50-year chart of the S&P 500.
- Shaded gray vertical bars illuminate (representing recessions).
- Red flashing markers showing negative headline quotes (*"Worst economy in decades"*, *"No end in sight"*).
- A sharp green arrow emerges from the very bottom of the gray recession band, shooting upward while the recession is still active.

---

## 2. The Core Question

> **"When you strip away political rhetoric and cable news panic, what does 50 years of empirical economic data reveal about how recessions actually start, how long they last, and where everyone makes fatal assumptions?"**

---

## 3. Narrative Arc (Three-Act Structure)

```
[ ACT 1: THE ILLUSION ] (0:00–2:15)
  • The Hook & The NBER Definition Myth
  • Why "two quarters of negative GDP" is not the real definition
  • The Lagging Referee: Why recessions are only declared when they're half over

[ ACT 2: THE 50-YEAR AUTOPSY ] (2:15–5:00)
  • 1973–75: The Oil Shock & The Inflation Trap
  • 1981–82: Volcker's 20% Interest Rate Hammer
  • 2001: The Tech Valuation Bubble
  • 2008 vs 2020: The Balance Sheet Freeze vs The 2-Month Shock
  • The Duration Paradox: Why the average recession lasts only 10 months, while expansions last 5–10 years

[ ACT 3: THE TWO ASYMMETRIES ] (5:00–6:45)
  • Asymmetry 1: The Stock Market Bottom (Leading vs Lagging)
    - Why waiting for "good news" guarantees missing the bottom
  • Asymmetry 2: The Job Market Scar (The Elevator Down, The Stairs Up)
    - Why the public still feels broke 3 years after the official recovery

[ CONCLUSION & THE SYSTEM ] (6:45–7:15)
  • The Big Takeaway: Recessions are systemic resets, not permanent destructions
  • Direct Outro: Call to action + Teaser for Week 3 (AI Recommendation Algorithms)
```

---

## 4. Target Audience & Viewer Takeaways

- **Viewer Feeling After Watching**: Grounded, enlightened, immune to news panic.
- **The "Aha!" Moment**: Understanding that the stock market discounts the future (6–9 months ahead), while economic statistics measure the past.
- **Retention Driver**: Chronological progression with visual data markers keep the viewer watching until the final recovery analysis.
