## 2. Key Empirical Insights & Packaging Takeaways

1. **The "Prediction Illusion" vs. "Mathematical Reality" Contrast**:
   - The highest-performing videos on financial AI consistently reject the "magic crystal ball" narrative. Viewers in this niche are sophisticated; clickbait promising 99% accuracy produces high initial clicks but catastrophic early drop-off.
   - Successful thumbnails focus on a **single stark mathematical truth** or a **counter-intuitive institutional metric** (e.g. 50.75% edge, alpha decay curve).

2. **Typography Hierarchy**:
   - High CTR thumbnails never exceed 2–3 words.
   - All-caps, ultra-bold sans-serif (`Inter` / `Segoe UI`) with high contrast against near-black backgrounds achieves immediate comprehension on mobile displays.

3. **Visual Composition & Focal Points**:
   - Single hero focal visual on the rule-of-thirds axis.
   - Avoid cluttered multi-subject collages. An illuminated mathematical element (e.g. a sharp Gaussian distribution line or a razor-thin gold wedge) beats generic stock trading screens.
