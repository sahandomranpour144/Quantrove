# Visual Style Standard (applies to all future long-form and Shorts)

## Background
Raisin Black (`#202322`) background on every scene — strictly adhering to 
the Institutional Data Intelligence palette defined in `brand/brand_tokens.json`. 
Canvases and dark surfaces must use `#202322`.

## Typography
- Clean modern sans-serif fonts only — no serif, no script, no decorative fonts
- **Primary Font**: `Nohemi` (with `Inter` fallback if Nohemi fails to load)
- **Bold weight (700)**: titles, numbers, key terms/keywords (including kinetic pop-ups)
- **Medium weight (500)**: body text, supporting captions, secondary information
- Never use a weight lighter than medium for anything on screen — readability 
  at small Shorts sizes fails below medium weight
- High-contrast Off-White (`#E6EDF3`) text against the dark background at all times — 
  no mid-tone or low-contrast text colors.
- Charcoal Slate (`#233D4C`) is for lines and chrome ONLY, never text.
- Power Lime (`#C3D809`) for keyword emphasis and positive validation.
- Pumpkin (`#FD802E`) for risk, anomalies, and outliers. Zero generic red/green.

## Pacing
Never leave a static frame (no motion, no new element, no text change) on 
screen longer than 3-4 seconds — unless it's a deliberate hold specifically 
to let the viewer process a complex idea, and even then state explicitly 
that it's a deliberate hold, not an oversight.

## Layout
Minimal clutter. Intentional negative space — don't fill empty frame area 
just because it's available. One clear focal point per scene.

## Applies to
Both long-form and Shorts, for all on-screen text: captions, kinetic 
pop-up keywords, and titles alike. This is a visual consistency rule, not 
a content-structure rule — it says nothing about pacing of ideas, scene 
count, or voiceover, only how things look once they're on screen.
