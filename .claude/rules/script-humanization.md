# Rule: Quantrove Documentary Script Humanization

## What the Rule Is
Every long-form script draft must pass the 4-pass documentary humanization workflow before submission to Sahand for Gate 1 approval.
- **Rule 1 (Banish AI Tropes)**: Purge all generic AI phrases ("In today's world", "rapidly evolving", "landscape", "revolutionary", "it is important to understand", "delve into").
- **Rule 2 (Documentary Chapter Quad)**: Every chapter must deliver: Human Question → Mystery / Problem → Data Reveal → Consequence & Meaning.
- **Rule 3 (Curiosity-Driven Framing)**: Replace clinical causal explanations ("The crash happened because...") with tension-driven curiosity pivots ("But something strange appears when we look at every crash together...").
- **Rule 4 (Investigative Observer Voice)**: The narrator speaks as a researcher uncovering system mechanics alongside the audience, not a textbook lecturer.
- **Rule 5 (Anti-Textbook Architecture)**: Forbid "Definition → Explanation → Example"; enforce "Problem → Unexpected pattern → Data reveal → Meaning".
- **Rule 6 (Quantitative Fidelity)**: Maintain 100% mathematical accuracy, exact data points, and neutral financial analysis.
- **Rule 7 (Zero Fabrication)**: Strictly forbid invented personal experiences, fake emotions, or unsubstantiated speculation.

## Bug / Incident Prevented
Unfiltered LLM scripts default to formulaic textbook explanations, sterile corporate clichés, and passive narration that collapses early YouTube retention.
