# Hook Discipline

## The rule
Every long-form episode must state the specific question or value proposition 
the video answers within the first 5 seconds — not a general topic intro, 
not channel branding, not a slow build-up. The viewer should know exactly what 
they're about to learn before the 5-second mark, and the first 5 seconds must 
include at least 2 primary title keywords spoken and on screen (per rule L2).

## Why this exists
Generic topic intros ("Today we're talking about market crashes...") lose 
retention in the first 10 seconds, which is the single highest drop-off window 
on YouTube. A specific question ("Why did the market lose 22% in one day in 
1987, and could it happen tomorrow?") gives the viewer a reason to stay.

## Check before scripting is considered done
Read the first 5 seconds of the script out loud. If a viewer couldn't state 
the video's specific promise and hear at least 2 title keywords after hearing only that, rewrite the opening.

## Applies to
Long-form only (Rule L2). Shorts enforce hook discipline via rule R1 (<0.5s first word, <=0.1s text, 3.0s window).
