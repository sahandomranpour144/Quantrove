### Candidate 2: *Diffusion Models: How Math Reverses Pure Noise*

- **Visual Opportunity**: Manim particle diffusion fields, forward Brownian motion, reverse denoising trajectory following vector gradients in a 2D energy landscape.
- **Data Opportunity**: Real forward/reverse Markov chain tensors, FID score benchmarks, noise schedule curves.

### Candidate 3: *The Double Descent Paradox: Why Overfitting Lies*
- **Central Question**: For 50 years, statistics taught that too many parameters destroy accuracy. Why do modern 70B parameter models get smarter the more they overparameterize?
- **Curiosity Mechanism**: The classical bias-variance trade-off mandates a U-shaped error curve. But modern deep learning breaks this law. At the interpolation threshold where parameters equal data points, error peaks—and then plunges into a "second descent."
- **Educational Value**: Explains why modern overparameterized models generalize, introducing implicit regularization, SGD dynamics, and Grokking.
